HERO MADE FIT

Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026. This policy takes effect on that date.

Hero Made Fit is a fitness app in development by Luke Horger. This page covers the health data we collect through Coach Tommy Ashman's fitness assessment (heromadefit.com/club/v3) and our hero assessment. It follows Washington's My Health My Data Act and Nevada's consumer health data law, and we follow it for everyone. Our full privacy policy is at heromadefit.com/privacy.html.

Health data we collect

  • injuries and conditions you tell us about, such as back, knee or shoulder problems
  • heart or blood pressure problems, and chest pain or dizziness when active
  • pregnancy, or having recently had a baby
  • how much you sleep and how stressed you are
  • food allergies and restrictions
  • fitness test results: reps, weights, times, jumps and distances, plus your age, sex, height and bodyweight, and the rank, stats and belts we work out from them
  • a photo for your hero art, if you choose one: a selfie or a full-body photo. The AI service that draws your hero may map the features of your face to do it.
  • the notes made from your answers: an AI summary and nutrition draft for Coach Tommy, and any plan he sends you

Only Coach Tommy's assessment asks the health questions (injuries, heart, pregnancy, sleep, stress and food). Both assessments ask for test results, and both offer a photo.

Why we collect it

  • Your health answers are used only to help Coach Tommy coach you safely: what to flag first (like getting your doctor's OK), your training plan and your nutrition plan.
  • Your test results are used to work out your rank, stats and belts, to show them on your own link, and to help Tommy coach you. Luke also uses them to improve the assessment's tests.
  • Your photo is used only to draw your hero picture, never to identify you.

We never sell your health data or use it for ads.

Where it comes from

From you, through the assessment. The rank, stats, belts and AI notes are worked out from your own answers.

Who we share it with

Your data is kept on Hero Made Fit's encrypted computer, which only Luke can open, and is shared with:

  • Coach Tommy Ashman (his assessment only): all your answers, including your health answers, your stats and hero art, and the AI notes, on his private dashboard. He doesn't see your photo. He can download a copy of your answers for his own records, without your photo or your health answers. That copy is his.
  • Google: your answers and any photo pass through Luke's private Google account. Your photo and the answers you sent are deleted there once they reach our computer. The copy Tommy's dashboard reads (all your answers, including health answers, and the AI notes), your results and hero art, and any plan Tommy sends you stay in that account until you or Tommy ask us to delete them. After a deletion, an older copy can stay in the Google Sheet's version history, which only Luke can see.
  • Anthropic: its AI, Claude, reads your answers (not your name or photo) to write Tommy's summary and nutrition draft. In the hero assessment, it reads the description of your best skill to score it.
  • Higgsfield and xAI, to draw your hero art: your photo, or the words you use to describe yourself, plus your sex, age range, height, build and the outfit you pick, and anything you type in the description or drawing box. They never get your name or your health answers. We've turned off Higgsfield's setting that lets it train on our uploads, but Higgsfield couldn't confirm it covers every model, and xAI handles uploads under its own terms.

No other company collects your health data on our pages, over time or across other websites. The assessments use no cookies, ad trackers or analytics.

Your rights

  • See your data: what we hold, whether we've shared it, a list of everyone who received it, and how to contact each of them.
  • Correct it if something's wrong.
  • Withdraw your consent: we stop collecting, using and sharing your health data.
  • Delete it: we delete it from our computer and from our Google account, and from our own backups within 6 months. We ask Higgsfield to delete your photo and hero art; Higgsfield reviews deletion requests under its own policy and may keep copies for a while. Anthropic and xAI keep what they received under their own policies and can't remove one person's data on request. Tommy's downloaded copy is his own records, so we let him know you asked.

How to use them

Email privacy@heromadefit.com and say what you'd like. Include your results link, or your first name and the day you took the assessment, so we can find your answers and check it's you. You can also ask Coach Tommy to delete your answers from his dashboard.

It's free. We reply within 30 days. A deletion is done within 30 days of confirming it's you. A copy in a backup we can't change that quickly is deleted within 6 months at most.

How to appeal

If we turn down your request, or don't do all of it, you can appeal. Reply to our answer, or email privacy@heromadefit.com with "Appeal" in the subject. We'll answer in writing within 30 days and explain why.

If we turn down your appeal, you can complain to your state's Attorney General:

  • Washington: atg.wa.gov/file-complaint, or call 1‑800‑551‑4636 (in Washington) or 206‑464‑6684.
  • Nevada: ag.nv.gov/Complaints/File_Complaint.

Changes

We won't use your health data in a new way without a fresh OK, asked in the assessment itself. When this page changes, we'll update the date above.
