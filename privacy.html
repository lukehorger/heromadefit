HERO MADE FIT

Privacy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Hero Made Fit is a fitness app in development by Luke Horger. This page covers the waitlist on heromadefit.com, our hero assessment, and the selfie option in it. It's short because we collect very little.

What we collect

Only what you type into the waitlist form:

  • your email address
  • how you train, which apps you use today, what you'd pay, and whether you use iPhone or Android
  • the link you came from, if it had a tag like ?ref=instagram, so we know which posts work
  • the date and time you signed up

We also count visits. When the page opens, it sends only its tag (or "direct"), which adds one to that day's count for that tag. To count you once a day, your browser keeps the date of your last visit; that stays on your device. We don't use cookies or ad trackers, and we don't record your IP address or browser details.

What we use it for

  • Emails about Hero Made Fit: updates while we build it, early access, the launch, and reminders after it.
  • Possibly an invitation to a short, optional chat about your training. Saying no is fine and changes nothing.
  • Invitations to join the Hero Made Fit community (for example, a Discord server) to share ideas and report bugs. Joining is optional, and it has its own privacy terms.
  • Counting signups and answers in total to decide whether to build the app.

We never sell your details, share them with advertisers, or add you to anyone else's list.

Where it's kept

Signups are stored in a private Google Sheet that only Luke can open, through Google Apps Script. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web host, GitHub and Google see your IP address when your browser loads the page, under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your details. If we decide not to build the app, we'll tell you and then delete the list.

Your choices

Email hello@heromadelabs.com from the address you signed up with to see what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Every email we send will also have an unsubscribe link.

Hero assessment

If you take the hero assessment from a link we sent you, we collect what you enter there:

  • your first name, and your full name if you sign the face data release for a selfie
  • your age and sex, and if you add them, your height, bodyweight, shirt size and build
  • the skills you pick and your test results: reps, weights and times, plus your longest run, ride and swim in the past year, and a description of your best skill, if you add them
  • how you'd like your hero drawn: a selfie (see below) or the words you use to describe yourself
  • any feedback you send from your results page

We use your answers to work out your rank, belts and next level-up, and to draw your hero. Your results and hero art show on your own link, so anyone you share that link with can see them. Nobody else can see them on the site. We also look at answers in total to improve the tests.

Your answers arrive through Google Apps Script. Google's copy of your answers, and any selfie, is deleted for good as soon as it reaches our computer, which is private and encrypted. So that your link can show them, a copy of your results and hero art stays in our Google account until your hero is deleted.

To draw your hero, Higgsfield, using xAI's image model, gets your selfie or your description, plus build words based on your height, weight and build. It never gets your name, and training is off on our account. If you describe your best skill, Anthropic's AI, Claude, reads that description, and any follow-up answers you give, to score it.

We keep your answers, results and hero until the app launches, so they can carry over. If we decide not to build the app, we delete them. Selfies follow the 30-day rule below. You can email privacy@heromadefit.com any time to see, correct or delete them.

The teen version works out your belts on your screen, and saves and sends nothing.

Face data (selfies)

If you choose to use a selfie for your hero, we collect that photo, and the AI service we use may map facial features from it. Some laws call this biometric data.

  • Purpose: only to draw your hero picture. We don't use face data to identify anyone, and we never sell, rent, trade or otherwise profit from it.
  • Who receives it: Higgsfield, the AI image service that draws the art, and xAI, the company behind the Grok drawing model Higgsfield uses, only with your written consent. On its way to us, the selfie passes through a private Google account (Google Drive), where it's permanently deleted as soon as it arrives. We've turned off Higgsfield's setting that lets it train on our uploads, but Higgsfield couldn't confirm it covers every model, and xAI handles photos under its own terms.
  • Retention and destruction: we permanently delete your selfie from our systems within 30 days after your hero is drawn, or sooner if you ask. In every case it's deleted within 3 years of your last interaction with us. At the same time, we ask Higgsfield to delete it. Higgsfield's handling is governed by its own policy. xAI's handling is governed by its own policy.
  • Protection: selfies are stored on an encrypted computer and sent only over encrypted connections. Only the person running Hero Made Fit can access them.
  • Your hero art and results are not face data. They're kept as described under Hero assessment above.
  • To ask for deletion: email privacy@heromadefit.com.
  • Ages: the selfie option is for people 18 and older.

Children

The waitlist is for people 13 and older. If you're under 13, please don't sign up.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date above. If the change affects how we use details you've already given us, we'll email you first.
